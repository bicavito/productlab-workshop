## The behavior change

What will users do differently after this feature ships?

**Sentence structure:**
> [Increase / Decrease] the [number / rate / frequency] of [user behavior X] in order to reach [product metric Y], so that [business outcome Z].

**Your answer:**
Increase the number of hiring managers who check pipeline status inside TalentFlow (instead of asking recruiters via email or Slack) from under 5% to 40% within 60 days, in order to reduce recruiter admin load by 30%, so that accounts retain and expand.

**Example:**
*"Increase the number of users who complete their first data export within 7 days of signup in order to reach a 60% day-30 retention rate, so that monthly churn drops below 6%."*

## The connection to the business outcome

How does this behavior change lead directly to the metric in `business_outcome.md`?

**Your answer (1 sentence):**
Accounts where hiring managers self-serve pipeline data have 3x lower churn because the reporting gap forces manual workarounds that erode trust and trigger competitor evaluations.

## What blocks it today

Why isn't this behavior already happening? What is the product limitation standing in the way?

**Your answer (1–2 sentences):**
There is no pipeline overview. Hiring managers cannot see where their candidates are in the process without asking the recruiter. The only view is a flat candidate list with no filtering by stage, job, or time period.
